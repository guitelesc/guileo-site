# 🚀 Growth / Retention — Guileo Company-OS

> The department that maximizes the lifetime value of every existing CRM contact: Gui autonomously scores churn risk, reactivates lapsed customers, and runs win-back, loyalty, upsell/cross-sell and referral loops off the same unified contact spine (crm_contacts + client_events + revenue ledger + biz_appointments) — turning one-time buyers into repeat high-LTV regulars at superhuman scale without the owner lifting a finger.

## 🗄️ Data model (entities this dept owns)
- **retention_scores** — `id, tenant_id, contact_id (FK crm_contacts, UNIQUE per tenant), churn_risk(0-1), predicted_ltv_cents, predicted_next_event_at, recency_days, frequency, monetary_cents, lifecycle_stage(new|active|at_ri`  
  ↳ Per-contact predictive layer — exactly one row per crm_contacts row. Recomputed nightly by automations cron AND instantly on every new client_event (payment, completed biz_appointment, call sentiment). churn_risk + lifecycle_stage are mirrored back onto crm_contacts so Sales/Service/Gui-at-call-time read the same truth. No data copied: RFM is derived from client_events + revenue ledger keyed by contact_id.
- **growth_segments** — `id, tenant_id, name, rule_json (RFM thresholds + crm tags + lifecycle_stage + last biz_appointment age), is_dynamic, materialized_view_name, contact_count, created_by(gui|owner), refreshed_at`  
  ↳ Live audiences expressed purely as a WHERE over the crm_contacts + retention_scores join (never a separate contact list). rpc_refresh_segments rematerializes membership so editing any contact anywhere instantly changes who is targeted. The targeting unit for every campaign.
- **growth_campaigns** — `id, tenant_id, type(reactivation|winback|loyalty|upsell|referral|milestone), segment_id (FK growth_segments), goal_metric, channel(voice|sms|whatsapp|email), offer_id (FK offers), schedule_json, statu`  
  ↳ Orchestrates a growth play. Its touches are emitted ONLY as gui_actions through the call.js dial gate (voice) or the channel senders, and outcomes are read back from client_events + revenue ledger keyed by contact_id — so a reactivation call sits on the same timeline as the original order.
- **campaign_enrollments** — `id, tenant_id, campaign_id (FK), contact_id (FK crm_contacts), state(queued|touched|responded|converted|snoozed|exited_dnc|exited_won), gui_action_id (FK gui_actions), converting_event_id (FK client_e`  
  ↳ The join that ties a contact to a campaign and makes outcomes attributable. One row per (contact, campaign); links the exact gui_action that fired and the exact client_event that converted, so recovered revenue maps to the precise person and play. Auto-exits on payment/DNC/missed-cap via the webhook.
- **loyalty_accounts** — `id, tenant_id, contact_id (FK crm_contacts, UNIQUE per tenant), points_balance, tier(bronze|silver|gold|vip), lifetime_points, points_to_next_tier, joined_at, last_earned_at`  
  ↳ One per contact. tier is auto-derived from revenue-ledger spend + frequency on crm_contacts, so loyalty status is always truthful and unified — no separate customer record. Mirrored to crm_contacts.loyalty_tier for call-time use.
- **loyalty_ledger** — `id, tenant_id, contact_id (FK crm_contacts), delta, reason(purchase|referral|bonus|redemption|expiry), ref_event_id (FK client_events), balance_after, idem_key, created_at`  
  ↳ Append-only points journal that mirrors the revenue-ledger pattern. Every earn/burn references the client_event that caused it (idem_key dedupes replays), so points are auditable and reconcile to the contact's real payment history.
- **offers** — `id, tenant_id, name, kind(percent|fixed|free_item|points_mult|bundle), value, eligibility_rule_json, redeem_code, max_redemptions, redemptions_used, expires_at, cost_to_business_cents`  
  ↳ Reusable incentive catalog with margin + cap guardrails. Campaigns reference an offer; redemptions post a NEGATIVE/discount line to the existing revenue ledger keyed by contact_id, so margin impact is measured on the same books as normal sales.
- **referrals** — `id, tenant_id, referrer_contact_id (FK crm_contacts), referred_contact_id (FK crm_contacts, nullable until join), status(invited|joined|converted|rewarded), referral_code (UNIQUE), reward_offer_id (FK`  
  ↳ Two-sided referral graph where BOTH ends are real crm_contacts rows (the referred party becomes a contact on first touch). Conversion is proven by linking the referred contact's first paid client_event in the revenue ledger; both reward payouts post back to loyalty_ledger / offers redemption.
- **expansion_opportunities** — `id, tenant_id, contact_id (FK crm_contacts), kind(upsell|cross_sell|reorder|upgrade), product_or_service, confidence, predicted_value_cents, source_pattern, status(open|pitched|won|lost), pitched_gui_`  
  ↳ Gui-generated next-best-offer per contact, derived from that contact's biz_appointments cadence + revenue-ledger order patterns + lookalike contacts in the same tenant. When pitched it spawns a gui_action; when won it links the converting client_event so attach-rate is measured, not guessed.

## 🟢 Core features (what the owner does)
- Open the Retention Dashboard in the Cockpit portal: at-risk contacts, predicted churn this month, recoverable revenue $, LTV trend, and which Gui plays are live — in aggregate and drillable to any single contact.
- Open any contact's existing 360 card and see an injected Growth panel: churn risk, predicted LTV, loyalty tier/points, next-best-offer, and every Gui growth touch inline on the same timeline as their calls, orders and biz_appointments.
- One-click launch (or approve a Gui-proposed) reactivation/win-back campaign on a dynamic segment — pick channel (voice/SMS/WhatsApp/email), offer, and guardrails (freq cap, quiet hours, DNC, min sentiment).
- Build and save dynamic RFM segments with a no-code rule builder ('no biz_appointment in 60d AND revenue-ledger spend > $300 AND tag=clinic') that auto-refresh as CRM data changes.
- Configure the loyalty program — tiers, point rules, rewards — and let Gui enroll, score, award and notify customers automatically off real payment history.
- Set up referral loops (codes, two-sided rewards) and watch the referral graph and ledger-attributed revenue grow in real time.
- Define/approve offers in a catalog with margin guardrails and redemption caps, then let Gui match the right offer to the right contact.
- Review and approve Gui's autonomous proposals (segments to wake up, offers to send, VIPs slipping) in a Cockpit approval inbox before they go live — or flip any play to full-auto.

## 🟣 Gui automations (how Gui runs it — superhuman)
- Re-scores every contact for churn risk, predicted LTV and predicted-next-event the instant a relevant client_event lands (new payment, completed/missed biz_appointment, call sentiment) AND in a nightly cron sweep — so the at-risk list is never stale and reacts in seconds, across the entire book, something no human team could do continuously.
- Learns each contact's PERSONAL cadence from their biz_appointments + order history and the moment someone slips past their own rhythm (not a generic 30-day rule) auto-enrolls them into the right win-back play — running this individualized vigilance over thousands of contacts at once, 24/7.
- Picks the next-best-offer AND the channel per contact from CRM history: the customer who only ever answers WhatsApp gets WhatsApp, the VIP gets a personal voice call from Gui through the call.js dial gate, the email-opener gets a branded email — then executes within guardrails.
- Runs an always-on lapsed queue: every morning surfaces 'lapsed 90d' contacts and works them via voice/SMS/WhatsApp/email until each is recovered, snoozed, or marked DNC — and when Gui recovers them it can book the return visit in the same conversation via the agent-schedule bridge.
- Manages loyalty end-to-end: awards points on every payment client_event (idempotently), promotes/demotes tiers off real spend, and nudges 'you're 50 points from Gold' at the optimal moment in the natural flow of the next call or message.
- Detects upsell/cross-sell/reorder windows ('your usual cut is due', 'X pairs with your last order') from biz_appointments cadence + revenue-ledger patterns and pitches them inside the next conversation Gui is already having — zero extra outreach cost.
- Closes referral loops autonomously: on high-sentiment calls Gui asks for a referral, issues a code, tracks the referred new contact to their first paid client_event in the revenue ledger, and pays out both rewards via loyalty_ledger without the owner touching anything.
- Self-optimizes per segment: A/B tests offer, timing and channel, learns which message recovers which customer type, writes the winner back into campaign guardrails, and reallocates effort toward what actually drives repeat revenue.
- Protects relationships at scale: every touch passes the call.js DNC + per-contact frequency-cap + quiet-hours + prior-sentiment gate, so high-value customers are never over-contacted or annoyed into churning — guardrails Gui enforces perfectly on every single send.

## 🔗 CRM-spine links (how it stays unified — zero silos)
- Reads the unified crm_contacts spine and its 360 timeline (client_events: calls, orders, biz_appointments, payments, messages) as the SOLE input for RFM, churn and LTV — every growth entity carries contact_id + tenant_id FKs into crm_contacts, so there is no second customer store.
- Writes retention_scores.churn_risk, lifecycle_stage and loyalty_accounts.tier back onto the crm_contacts record (mirror columns), so Sales, Service and Gui-at-call-time all see identical, instantly-updated truth.
- Every Gui growth touch is emitted as a gui_action and logged as a client_event on the contact timeline (campaign_enrollments.gui_action_id links them), so a reactivation call appears alongside the original order as one continuous history.
- Subscribes to client_events (new payment, completed/missed biz_appointment, call sentiment) via /api/growth/webhook to trigger instant re-scoring and auto-enroll / auto-exit contacts from campaigns in real time.
- Offer redemptions, referral conversions and loyalty earns post to the EXISTING revenue ledger and reference the exact client_event id, so recovered and expansion revenue is attributed to the precise contact and play on the same books as normal sales.
- growth_segments and loyalty tiers are materialized VIEWS over the crm_contacts + retention_scores join (never copies), so a change to a contact in any other department immediately reflects in who gets targeted and what tier they hold.
- All reads/writes are tenant-scoped by the existing per-tenant RLS in project ynihodbknhjirkdogxby (anon insert-only on gui_actions, SECURITY-DEFINER RPCs gated by secret), so one business's growth data can never leak into another's.

## 📈 KPIs it moves
- Reactivation rate (% of lapsed contacts returned to active) and recovered revenue $ (attributed via campaign_enrollments.converting_event_id)
- Churn rate / retention rate by cohort and lifecycle_stage
- Repeat-purchase rate and average purchase frequency per contact
- Customer LTV and LTV growth vs. baseline (predicted_ltv vs realized revenue-ledger)
- Win-back conversion rate and median time-to-reactivate
- Referral count, referral conversion rate, and referral-attributed revenue
- Loyalty enrollment %, active-member rate, and redemption rate
- Upsell/cross-sell attach rate and average-order-value lift
