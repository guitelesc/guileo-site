# 📊 Intelligence / Analytics — Guileo Company-OS

> The always-on brain of the company-OS: it reads the CRM contact spine (client_events + biz_appointments + the revenue ledger that hang off every contact) as its only source of truth, turns that unified 360° timeline into a live picture of the business, predicts what's about to happen (no-shows, churn, demand surges, dead slots, LTV), writes those predictions back onto each contact so every department sees the same scores, then tells the owner and Gui the single highest-leverage next move — fired through the same gui_actions funnel as the rest of the OS — and self-grades whether it worked.

## 🗄️ Data model (entities this dept owns)
- **intel_metrics_daily** — `tenant_id, metric_date, metric_key (revenue|bookings|no_show_rate|new_contacts|reactivations|avg_ticket|answer_rate|occupancy|ltv), value_num, dims jsonb (service/professional/channel/location), sourc`  
  ↳ Pre-aggregated nightly rollup of the whole tenant by day+dimension. NOT a siloed copy: every row records which client_events kinds + biz_appointments + revenue-ledger rows it summed (source_event_kinds) and keeps a contact_ids_sample so any metric drills straight back down to the exact CRM contacts behind it. Fuel for every dashboard/trend.
- **intel_predictions** — `tenant_id, contact_id (FK contacts), subject_type (contact|appointment|demand_slot), subject_id, model_key (no_show|churn|ltv|demand|reactivation), score (0-1), band (low|med|high), top_factors jsonb,`  
  ↳ One row per scored entity, ALWAYS keyed to a contact_id (demand_slot rows carry the cohort of affected contact_ids in top_factors). The current/latest score per (contact,model) is mirrored onto the contact record itself (see crmLinks); written_to_contact confirms the spine is in sync — no prediction lives only here.
- **intel_insights** — `tenant_id, insight_id, kind (anomaly|trend|opportunity|risk), title, narrative, severity (1-5), confidence, expected_revenue_impact_num, evidence jsonb (metric_date+metric_key refs + contact_ids[]), r`  
  ↳ The 'so what' layer Gui mines from metrics+predictions. evidence always cites real contact_ids so the owner can open the exact people behind any finding; recommended_action is a ready-to-run gui_actions payload, and acting stamps gui_action_id so the insight and the executed action are one linked record, never a parallel side-channel.
- **intel_briefs** — `tenant_id, period (weekly|monthly), period_start, period_end, summary_md, wins jsonb, losses jsonb, top_insights jsonb (insight_ids), kpi_snapshot jsonb, contact_highlights jsonb (won-back/at-risk/top`  
  ↳ Immutable weekly/monthly executive brief for week-over-week compare. contact_highlights pins the named people behind the numbers (the 9 slipping regulars, the top spenders) so the brief is clickable into the CRM, and email_template_key='intel_weekly_brief' enforces the branded-emails-only rule.
- **intel_experiments** — `tenant_id, experiment_id, hypothesis, lever (reminder_copy|offer|deposit_pct|call_time|followup_cadence), variant_a jsonb, variant_b jsonb, target_metric, allocation, status (running|paused|won|lost|i`  
  ↳ A/B registry for any tenant-facing lever Gui controls. Enrolled contacts are NOT copied into a list — they are referenced by contact_id in the intel_experiment_assignments join (below), so every variant cohort is just a live view over the CRM and can never drift out of sync with it.
- **intel_experiment_assignments** — `tenant_id, experiment_id (FK), contact_id (FK contacts), variant (a|b), assigned_at, exposed_at, converted bool, conversion_event_id (FK client_events), client_event_id (FK client_events for the exper`  
  ↳ The thin join that keeps A/B inside the CRM spine: one row per (experiment, contact). Assignment writes an experiment_assigned client_event onto the contact's timeline, and conversion points at the actual client_events row (booking/payment) that resolved it — so significance is computed from real timeline events, not a separate tally.
- **intel_model_runs** — `tenant_id, model_key, run_id, run_at, rows_scored, contacts_scored, calibration jsonb (auc/brier vs realized outcomes from client_events/biz_appointments), drift_flag, threshold_applied, notes`  
  ↳ Audit + self-grading log. Each run resolves prior predictions against what actually happened on the spine (no_show predicted vs the biz_appointments status, churn predicted vs whether a client_event reappeared), recalibrates the per-tenant threshold, and flags drift — so the owner can trust the numbers and Gui keeps the models honest with zero data-science work.

## 🟢 Core features (what the owner does)
- Open one unified Intelligence dashboard in the Cockpit portal that pulls every department into a single live view — revenue, bookings, occupancy, answer rate, new vs returning contacts, cash collected, avg ticket — with drill-down by service, staff, channel and location, every tile sourced from the same CRM spine and clickable down to the exact contacts behind it
- See a Risk Board: appointments flagged high no-show, contacts flagged high churn, and slow upcoming slots flagged low-demand — each showing the predicted %, the top 3 reasons, and the affected contact(s) by name pulled straight from the contact record
- Read the Monday weekly brief in plain language and the owner's language ('You did R$X, up 12%; Tuesdays are dying; here are the 9 regulars slipping away — tap to win them back') delivered to email/WhatsApp/portal, with every named contact linking into the CRM timeline
- Act on any insight with one click — 'Win back these 9 contacts' fires Gui's outreach, 'Overbook Tuesday 2pm by 1' adjusts capacity — without leaving the dashboard, and the click is recorded as a gui_action plus a client_event on each affected contact
- Launch and watch A/B tests on reminder copy, offers, deposit %, call timing and follow-up cadence; enrolled contacts stay in the CRM (just tagged), and you see which variant wins with real lift measured from actual timeline conversions, not a guess
- Ask the business a question in natural language ('which service makes the most margin?', 'who hasn't come back in 60 days?', 'who are my top 10 LTV clients?') and get an answer that resolves to real contact_ids you can open and act on
- Set targets (monthly revenue, occupancy %) and get pace-to-goal tracking with an early warning the moment the month is trending to miss, plus the specific best-next-action to close the gap

## 🟣 Gui automations (how Gui runs it — superhuman)
- Every night before the owner wakes, Gui re-aggregates the ENTIRE tenant from client_events + biz_appointments + the revenue ledger into intel_metrics_daily, recomputes per-tenant anomaly baselines, and runs a full company audit — a daily closing of the books no human team would do for a single small business, every single day
- The instant an appointment is booked (and continuously as the date nears) Gui scores it for no-show risk from that contact's own history, lead time, channel, prior no-shows and sentiment, writes contact.no_show_risk back onto the spine, and auto-fires a smart confirmation or deposit request on the high-risk ones through the automations cron + call/message funnel — pre-empting losses a human only discovers when the chair sits empty
- Gui watches every contact in parallel against their OWN personal cadence (not a blanket rule), detecting churn the moment a regular's gap stretches past their normal rhythm, their frequency drops, or call sentiment turns negative — then queues win-back outreach the same hour, catching slippage months before a human would ever notice, across the whole book at once
- Gui forecasts demand per future slot and day and load-balances the calendar around the clock — surfacing 'open these two hours Thursday, the data says they'll fill' or 'block Monday morning, it's predictably dead' — turning the schedule into a yield-managed asset instead of a static grid
- Gui mines fresh metrics + predictions hourly, ranks every candidate insight by expected_revenue_impact × confidence, suppresses the noise, and shows the owner only the two or three findings that actually move money — superhuman triage across thousands of contacts and events that a person could never scan, let alone rank
- Gui writes and sends the weekly/monthly brief autonomously in the owner's language and concierge voice, names the real contacts behind each win and loss, and attaches a single ready-to-run best-next-action — so reading the brief and acting on it is one tap
- Gui self-grades every run: it resolves last period's predictions against what actually happened on the spine (no-show predicted vs appointment status, churn predicted vs whether the contact came back), recalibrates each tenant's thresholds, and flags model drift in intel_model_runs — the analytics get sharper the longer the business runs, with zero data-science work from the owner
- Gui auto-runs A/B tests in the background (e.g. two reminder scripts or two deposit %), reads significance off real timeline conversions, promotes the winner to the tenant default, retires the loser, and reports the lift — a continuous optimization loop on every customer-facing lever that never sleeps and never has to be told to start

## 🔗 CRM-spine links (how it stays unified — zero silos)
- READS the unified contact timeline as its sole source of truth: every metric, prediction and insight is computed from the same client_events (calls/messages/orders/payments), biz_appointments and revenue-ledger rows that hang off each CRM contact — intel_metrics_daily even stores source_event_kinds + a contact_ids_sample so nothing is recomputed from, or drifts away from, a siloed copy
- WRITES predictions back onto the contact record as first-class columns (contacts.churn_risk, contacts.ltv_pred, contacts.no_show_risk, contacts.segment, contacts.risk_updated_at) so every other department reads the same scores from one place — Gui greets a high-LTV contact differently, Sales prioritizes high-churn-risk, Scheduling guards high-no-show appointments, all off one spine
- WRITES each acted-on insight and every A/B enrollment as a client_event on the affected contact(s) (kind=intel_action with the insight_id, kind=experiment_assigned with the variant), so the 360° timeline reads as one continuous story: 'flagged churn-risk → win-back sent → rebooked' or 'assigned variant B reminder → confirmed → showed'
- EMITS every owner action and Gui automation through the existing gui_actions store (action_type=intel_no_show_guard / intel_winback / intel_overbook / intel_brief_send / intel_experiment_step), and stamps the resulting gui_action_id back onto the intel_insights row — keeping Intelligence inside the same action+audit+call/message pipeline as the rest of the OS, with no parallel channel
- ENROLLS and segments contacts IN PLACE: intel_experiment_assignments and segment tags are just references to contact_id, so A/B variant cohorts and segments are live views over the CRM that resolve through the contact spine — never a separate list that has to be kept in sync, and conversions point at the actual client_events row that resolved them

## 📈 KPIs it moves
- No-show rate (target: down — via predictive confirmations/deposits auto-fired on high-no_show_risk appointments)
- Churn / reactivation rate (target: more dormant regulars won back before they're gone — measured by contacts crossing back over their personal-cadence threshold)
- Revenue per available slot-hour and occupancy % (target: up — via demand forecasting and calendar load-balancing)
- Average ticket and contact LTV (target: up — via segment-aware best-next-action and upsell, tracked off contacts.ltv_pred vs realized)
- Answer/conversion rate from lead to booking (target: up — surfaced on the dashboard and optimized via A/B on call timing and reminder copy)
- Pace-to-target attainment (target: % of tenants hitting their monthly revenue/occupancy goal, with early-warning fired before month-end)
- Prediction accuracy / model trust (target: AUC & Brier up, drift_flag down in intel_model_runs — the system grading itself so the owner can rely on it)
