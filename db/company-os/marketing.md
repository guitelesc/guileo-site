# 📣 Marketing — Guileo Company-OS

> The top-of-funnel engine that manufactures demand: Gui hunts, attracts, and captures strangers across every channel (ads, SEO, content, reviews, referrals, social, email/SMS) and writes each one into the SAME unified CRM contact spine (gui_actions/client_events) that Sales and Performance already work from — so every lead is born already attributed (campaign/creative/utm stamped on its timeline), scored, deduped against existing contacts, and instantly handed to call.js for speed-to-lead. Marketing never owns a separate lead list; it is a layer of channels + attribution wrapped around the one contact record.

## 🗄️ Data model (entities this dept owns)
- **mkt_campaigns** — `id, client_email, name, objective(leadgen|awareness|reactivation|reviews|referral), channel(meta|google|tiktok|email|sms|seo|organic), status(draft|live|paused|done), budget_cents, daily_cap_cents, sp`  
  ↳ One row per campaign across any channel; budget + live pacing counters live here so the automations cron (same one that runs blitz/health) can throttle fail-closed like call.js rate-limits. Tenant-scoped by client_email and RLS-protected exactly like gui_actions (anon cannot SELECT; reads go through a SECURITY-DEFINER RPC). target_segment points at the CRM query that defines the audience, so the campaign is bound to real contacts, never an exported list.
- **mkt_creatives** — `id, campaign_id, client_email, kind(image|video|copy|landing|email|sms|post), headline, body, media_url, cta, variant_label(A|B|C), state(draft|pending_approval|approved|live|losing|winning|killed), i`  
  ↳ Every ad/post/email/SMS variant Gui generates; metrics roll up from mkt_attribution so Gui auto-kills losers and scales winners on the cron tick. The true win signal is booked+revenue_cents (joined back from biz_appointments + the revenue ledger via contact_id), NOT clicks — closing the loop ad platforms can't. Owner approval gate = state flips draft→approved before any spend/publish, mirroring the consent gate philosophy.
- **mkt_channels** — `id, client_email, channel, external_account_id, status(connected|expired|error), tokens_ref(pointer into app_secrets, never the literal token), last_sync_at, health jsonb`  
  ↳ Per-tenant connection registry for Meta/Google/GBP/TikTok/email/SMS — mirrors the existing client_integrations table pattern. Tokens live server-side in app_secrets (same place admin_token/secrets live), referenced by pointer, never shipped in the bundle — same secret-handling discipline as INGEST_SECRET/SCHED_SECRET.
- **mkt_attribution** — `id, contact_id(→ CRM contact / gui_actions identity, the spine), client_email, campaign_id, creative_id, channel, touch_type(first|assist|last), utm jsonb, landing_url, referrer, cost_cents, revenue_e`  
  ↳ THE join table between a CRM contact and what brought them in + what they became. Multi-touch (first/assist/last) so Gui computes true CAC, ROAS, and LTV-by-source on unified data. Written at lead capture (first touch) and again when biz_appointments/the revenue ledger fires (closing touch) — so every contact card shows its acquisition cost and lifetime value side by side with its calls and bookings.
- **mkt_content** — `id, client_email, type(blog|seo_page|gbp_post|social|review_reply), slug, title, body_md, keywords text[], target_city, status(draft|scheduled|published), publish_at, organic_views, organic_leads, org`  
  ↳ Owns the SEO/organic surface and EXTENDS the existing geo generators (/ai-receptionist/:city and /for/:slug from verticals.js) so organic traffic is a measured, attributable channel. Each organic lead captured from a page is written to the CRM with this content row stamped as first-touch in mkt_attribution (organic_contact_ids back-links the contacts it produced), so SEO bookings are provable, not a side project.
- **mkt_referrals** — `id, client_email, referrer_contact_id(→ CRM), referred_contact_id(→ CRM), code, status(sent|clicked|converted|rewarded), reward_cents, revenue_event_id(→ biz_appointments/ledger), channel, created_at,`  
  ↳ Turns existing happy CRM contacts into a lead source; BOTH ends are CRM contacts, so the referral lives on both timelines. Reward fires only after mkt_referral_reward validates a REAL revenue event (a confirmed biz_appointments or a ledger payment), never on a click — the same fail-closed, prove-before-pay discipline as the rest of the platform.
- **mkt_reviews** — `id, client_email, contact_id(→ CRM), booking_id(→ biz_appointments), platform(google|yelp|fb), rating, body, sentiment(😊|😐|😞), review_url, reply_state(none|drafted|posted), reply_body, requested_at, r`  
  ↳ Reputation as a lead driver, contact- AND booking-linked. Review requests are triggered off a completed biz_appointments / ledger payment (the same events that fire confirmations), and replies are drafted by Gui reusing the SAME sentiment model already running on call_logs transcripts — one sentiment brain across calls and reviews on the contact's single timeline.

## 🟢 Core features (what the owner does)
- Set a goal in plain language ('quero 20 agendamentos/semana, budget $1.5k/mês') and Gui proposes the full multi-channel plan — campaigns, CRM-segment audiences, creatives, landing pages, daily caps — bound to real contact segments, for one-click approval.
- Approve/reject Gui-generated creatives and copy in a review queue (state pending_approval→approved gate) before a single cent spends or anything posts publicly — same gate discipline as the TCPA consent checkbox.
- See one Marketing tab in the Cockpit portal (Portal.jsx, Aman-calm): spend, leads, CPL, ROAS, and which campaign/creative each BOOKED customer came from — every row deep-links straight into that contact's existing 360° CRM timeline (calls, bookings, payments, reviews).
- Connect channels (Meta/Google/Google Business Profile/TikTok/email/SMS) through a guided connect-UI that mirrors the existing client_integrations flow; revoke or pause any channel instantly (tokens stay in app_secrets).
- Launch a Reactivation / Referral / Review campaign against existing CRM segments (dormant_contacts, top-LTV, recent 5-star bookings) with one button — reusing the dormant_contacts table the blitz engine already populates.
- Set guardrails — monthly budget ceiling, blackout hours (reuse business_hours_only), brand do/don't, approval-required toggle — and let Gui operate autonomously inside them, fail-closed.
- Get a weekly owner email (branded, via emailTemplates.js, sent by the same cron worker as the health/digest emails): what Gui spent, what it earned (CRM-verified revenue), which creatives it killed/scaled, and the ONE decision it wants from you.

## 🟣 Gui automations (how Gui runs it — superhuman)
- Never-sleeps media buyer: the EXISTING automations cron (the one running blitz every 15min + health Mondays) gains an mkt-optimizer tick that reads live spend/leads/booked-revenue per creative from mkt_attribution, auto-pauses any variant whose CPL or CAC exceeds target, and shifts budget to winners — rebalancing every 15 min, 24/7, across every connected channel at once. No human ever watches the dashboard.
- Revenue-closed creative factory: Gui generates dozens of ad/copy/email/SMS variants per campaign, launches small-budget A/B/n tests, then reads the CRM (biz_appointments + revenue ledger via contact_id) to see which variants produced actual BOOKED REVENUE — not just clicks — and promotes the true revenue-winner. This is the loop Meta/Google literally cannot run because they never see the booking or the payment; Guileo does because it owns the spine.
- Predictive audience from outcomes: Gui mines the CRM spine for who actually became high-LTV customers (traits, source, vertical), and auto-refreshes lookalike/intent audiences from those real winners every week via mkt_segment — so targeting compounds instead of going stale, and the worst-performing 10% of audience is pruned automatically.
- Programmatic SEO at scale: a daily mkt-organic cron tick has Gui write and publish hundreds of city/service/vertical pages and GBP posts (extending the live /ai-receptionist/:city + /for/:slug generators), track organic_leads per page, and automatically rewrite any page that ranks but doesn't convert — turning organic into a self-tuning channel.
- Sub-60s speed-to-lead fusion: the instant any campaign captures a lead, mkt_capture_lead writes it to the CRM (gui_actions/client_events) and triggers the SAME call.js dial gate (E.164 + per-IP caps + DNC/consent honored) so Marketing's lead is dialed by Gui in ~60s — Marketing and Sales fuse with zero human handoff, and the call's outcome+sentiment flow back onto the same contact via call_logs.
- Reputation engine on autopilot: on every completed biz_appointments / ledger payment, Gui auto-sends a branded review request to the happy customer, drafts on-brand replies to incoming reviews using the SAME call-transcript sentiment model, and escalates any 😞 review to the owner's weekly email (or instantly for 1-star) before it spreads.
- Referral harvester from satisfaction signals: Gui detects high-satisfaction contacts (post-call 😊 in call_logs + repeat bookings in biz_appointments), sends personalized referral invites with tracked codes, and pays the reward ONLY after mkt_referral_reward confirms a real revenue event for the referred contact — fraud-proof by construction.
- Budget-safe by construction: every spend action checks the campaign daily_cap and tenant monthly ceiling fail-closed BEFORE acting — mirroring the call.js rate-limit/DNC gate philosophy and the per-phone cooldown — so Gui runs fully autonomously and can never overspend even under a bug or abuse.

## 🔗 CRM-spine links (how it stays unified — zero silos)
- Born attributed: every captured lead is written as a CRM contact through the EXISTING gui_actions/client_events ingest the moment it lands (mkt_capture_lead RPC, deduped against existing contacts by phone/email), with first-touch campaign_id, creative_id, channel and utm stamped onto the contact's timeline — never a siloed marketing list.
- CRM as the targeting brain: audiences are mkt_segment queries OVER the same contact spine (dormant_contacts, top-LTV, recent 5-star, lapsed-by-vertical), reusing the dormant_contacts table the blitz engine already maintains — so Reactivation/Referral/Review campaigns hit real records, not exports.
- Outcomes written back onto the contact 360°: when Sales books (biz_appointments) or the revenue ledger records a payment, mkt_attribute_revenue links that exact event to the originating campaign/creative — so CAC, ROAS and LTV-by-source are computed on unified data and shown right on the contact card next to its calls, orders and payments.
- Reviews and referrals are contact-linked both ways (referrer_contact_id + referred_contact_id, review contact_id + booking_id), and the review sentiment reuses the call_logs sentiment model — so a customer's word-of-mouth and reputation actions live on their single timeline alongside calls, bookings and payments: one record, full 360°, one sentiment brain.

## 📈 KPIs it moves
- Cost per lead (CPL) and cost per booked appointment, per channel and per creative (booked pulled from biz_appointments, not platform-reported)
- ROAS / blended CAC vs LTV by acquisition source, computed from the CRM revenue ledger — not platform-reported numbers
- Net-new CRM contacts per week and % reaching speed-to-lead dial in <60s (measured off the call.js timestamp)
- Organic lead share + SEO pages indexed/ranking and organic-sourced bookings (organic_contact_ids → biz_appointments)
- Review volume, average rating, and request→review conversion rate (request fired off completed bookings)
- Referral conversion rate and reward-validated revenue from referred contacts (only counts confirmed revenue events)
