## Competitor landscape — 2026-08-21 (Meta Ad Library, US, "AI receptionist missed calls", 674 active ads)

The niche is crowded and YOUNG: dozens of small pages, most ads created within days of the search — heavy testing, few visible scaled winners. Notable advertisers: ETollFree (4+ active variants — the most duplication seen = likely finding traction), FullCap (vertical per-niche ads: barbershops), RingDental / TensorLinks / VoxHealth / Polsia / Confido Health (dental & medical vertical players), HVAC Inbox / Flowserv / ResQ / Handraiser (home-services vertical), ElevenLabs (free-trial platform play), The Koerner Office ("The cheapest hire you'll ever make").

**Angles the market repeats (validated demand, expensive attention):**
1. "Never Miss Another Call" — the crowded default (ETollFree, FullCap, UniBright, AI Automation NY). Saturated; running this headline means blending in.
2. After-hours / late-night leak — "Stop the After-Hours Revenue Leak", "Tired of Late-Night Calls?" (ETollFree, HVAC Inbox).
3. Vertical-specific ads (one ad per niche: dental chair, barbershop, plumbing jobs) — the market's clear structural move; confirms our per-vertical creative plan.
4. Quantified guarantees — "30 Appointments, Guaranteed" (Frontdeskbots), "Recover 60 plumbing jobs in 90 days" (Flowserv).
5. Cheap-hire framing — "The cheapest hire you'll ever make" (Koerner) — adjacent to our master sentence.

**Angles NOBODY is running (the openings):**
- **The live demo** — no one says "the AI will call YOU right now." Every competitor asks for a form or trial. This is our moat (site already does it) and it is an empty lane, not a graveyard — it demos the exact product promise instantly.
- **Collects on the call** — everyone stops at "answers & books"; nobody claims payment collection. Ours does (pay-link by SMS, deposit-to-book).
- **Done-for-you agency (high ticket)** — the field sells self-serve SaaS + free trials. "We study your business and build it for you, run by our team" is unoccupied premium positioning.
- **Multilingual / immigrant-market** — zero ads seen in any language but English targeting owner communities (PT/ES). Blue-ocean flank for NYC/Miami.
