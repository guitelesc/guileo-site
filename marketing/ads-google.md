## Negative Keyword Suggestions

Add these to avoid wasted spend from low-intent or off-target searches:

**General negatives (apply to all campaigns):**
- free
- jobs / hiring / careers / salary
- how to (DIY / tutorial intent)
- diy
- meaning / definition / what is
- reddit
- crack / cracked / torrent
- review (if you only want buyers, not researchers — test first)
- internship
- course / training / certification

**Product-mismatch negatives:**
- human receptionist agency (if you want to attract those open to AI)
- call center jobs
- answering machine (the device)
- voicemail greeting
- google voice (brand researchers)
- twilio / vapi (developers wanting to build their own)

**Vertical-specific examples:**
- Dental: "dental school", "dental assistant jobs", "dental insurance"
- Restaurants: "restaurant jobs", "menu", "recipes", "near me open now" (diner intent, not owner)
- Law: "free legal advice", "pro bono", "law school", "lawyer jobs"

**Tip:** Review the search-terms report weekly for the first month and add anything irrelevant. Separate brand vs. non-brand campaigns so you can bid differently.
