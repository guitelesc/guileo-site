Google thinks your Twitter
belongs to Shopify.

I looked at capizzinyc.com today from the outside, the way a customer would. The site is handsome and the menu reads beautifully. But the block that tells Google who you are was never filled in — it still carries the theme's demo data. Below is what I measured, and the replacement, written for your restaurant, free to paste.

Measured today

Your structured data names someone else's social accounts as yours.

This is the sameAs block live on your homepage right now — it is how Google links a business to its official profiles:

"//www.facebook.com/ugmedia" ← not yours
"//twitter.com/shopify" ← Shopify's own account
"…instagram.com/capizzinyc/?hl=enenter url" ← "enter url" still pasted in

The Instagram link does still open — Instagram ignores the malformed part — so nothing is visibly broken to a customer. But to Google, Capizzi's official Facebook is a web agency's page and Capizzi's official Twitter is Shopify. That is the theme's placeholder content, never replaced.

Measured today

Google is told you are an “Organization”, not a restaurant.

Your markup says "@type": "Organization". A pizzeria is a Restaurant — and the difference is not cosmetic. Your full menu is published, your hours are published, your address is on every page, and none of it is marked up. So none of it can appear in the results that show hours, cuisine, price range or an order button. Your "url" is also just "/", which points nowhere from outside the page.

Measured today

Two different phone numbers, and neither one can be tapped.

Your header shows (646) 476-5120. Your footer shows (212) 695-6900. Both are plain text — there is not a single tap-to-call link anywhere on the site, and no email link either.

More than half of the people reading your menu are holding a phone. Right now, calling you means memorising a number and choosing between two.

Yours to keep

The replacement markup, written for Capizzi.

Built from what is published on your own site: the 547 9th Ave address, your real hours including the 3:30–5pm break, your menu page, your actual Instagram, and your DoorDash ordering link as a proper order action.

I left telephone empty on purpose — your site shows two different numbers and I am not going to guess which one rings the restaurant and publish it in your name. Put the right one in and it is done.
